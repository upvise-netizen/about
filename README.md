# Hi, I'm Sebastian

I build the systems between marketing and sales. Mostly in HubSpot, sometimes in Salesforce or Braze, and more and more with AI agents on top.

I'm based in Stockholm and have spent about 15 years in B2B marketing and operations. The part I still enjoy most is building things myself.

## What I build
- **CRM and RevOps setups:** data models, lifecycle stages, lead routing and the handoff from marketing to sales.
- **AI on CRM data:** lead qualification, enrichment and email copy tailored per lead, running inside HubSpot.
- **My own agent setup:** Claude Code with custom skills and agents for research, writing and CRM admin. Partly to save time, mostly to find out how far it goes before it breaks.

## One recent build
An AI SDR inside HubSpot. It checks every inbound lead against the ICP, fills the qualification properties and syncs the result to Salesforce. One master query feeds all the properties, which uses far fewer Breeze credits than a separate prompt per property. The customer stays anonymous. The pattern is free to steal.

## How I work
- Strategy and data model first, build second. AI on a messy CRM produces confident nonsense at scale.
- Test manually, then with a small audience, then release. Never on a Friday afternoon.
- Done means tested, monitored, documented and with a feedback loop in place.

## Opinions I'll defend over coffee
- HubSpot is the better choice for most mid-market SaaS companies.
- Last-click attribution doesn't hold up in B2B deals with long sales cycles and big buying committees.
- Marketing and sales should work from the same data. Most of my projects start because they don't.
